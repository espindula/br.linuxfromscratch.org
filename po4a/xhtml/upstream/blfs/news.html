BLFS 13.0 has been released!
Bruce Dubbs - 2026/03/05

The BLFS Team is proud to present version 13.0 of Beyond Linux From Scratch. This version includes over 1000 packages beyond the base Linux From Scratch Version 13.0 book. The book has over 1500 updates from the previous version including many text and formatting changes. This release also includes 112 security updates over the previous release.

The book includes the GNOME, KDE Plasma, XFCE, and LXQt Desktop environments. It also includes many applications including browsers, email clients, video players, and office applications.

New packages that were added include: Glycin sandboxed image loader, Showtime - GNOME video player, Loupe - GNOME image viewer (replacing EOG), simdutf (needed for VTE), kquickimageeditor and qtkeychain (needed for Plasma), kddockwidgets (needed for Kdenlive), lxqt-wayland-session (adding Wayland support to LXQt), libheif and libde265 (needed for Glycin), libfyaml (needed for AppStream), SVT-AV1 and dav1d (adding additional support for AV1 encoding and decoding), SDL3 and sdl2-compat (replacing SDL2 with significantly improved support for Wayland, input devices, and more), Maturin, setuptools_rust, semantic_version, and uv_build (Python modules needed for CacheControl), and blueprint-compiler (Python module) used by GNOME applications. Please see the ChangeLog in the book for a full list of changes.

The following packages have been removed: atkmm-2.28.x (not needed with gtkmm-4), dhcpcd (not needed in systemd), bridge-utils (not needed in systemd), pcre2 and sqlite (moved to LFS), Sendmail (not needed with Exim and Postfix), and webp-pixbuf-loader (not needed with glycin)

You can view the 13.0 systemd version at online systemd or download-systemd to read locally.
